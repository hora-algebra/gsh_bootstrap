# Claims Ledger

Every nontrivial statement used by the project belongs here. The status labels are:

- `PROVED`: proved in this repository, with a file/theorem location.
- `CITED`: taken from a named source, with theorem/page information where available.
- `COMPUTED`: established by a deterministic program that **decides the whole claim**: the
  statement is reduced to a finite object and that object is traversed exhaustively (product-
  automaton reachability, complete enumeration, full transition-monoid search). Logically
  complete; the only residual risk is a bug in the program.
- `EMPIRICAL`: checked by a deterministic program on a **finite sample** of the claim —
  bounded word length, random words, spot checks. **This is not evidence for the claim.**
  It can refute (a mismatch is a counterexample) but it can never establish, because the
  untested part of the domain is infinite. An `EMPIRICAL` row is a lead, not a result.
- `CONJECTURAL`: a precise mathematical conjecture.
- `SPECULATIVE`: a research direction not yet precise enough to be a conjecture.
- `REFUTED`: false; counterexample recorded.
- `UNREVIEWED`: imported or generated statement awaiting audit.

Do not upgrade a status by editing prose. Add the verification artifact.

**Status is the ceiling, and it propagates.** A row is at most as strong as its weakest
input. If any row it depends on is `EMPIRICAL`, this row is `EMPIRICAL` too, and its
evidence field must name the specific incomplete step — not merely cite the weaker row.
`scripts/lint_claims.py` enforces the `COMPUTED`/`EMPIRICAL` split mechanically.

**Vocabulary is bound to status.** Never write "settled", "resolved", "closed", "決着",
"解決" — in this file, in `RESULTS.md`, in `README.md`, or in a talk — for a claim whose
status is `EMPIRICAL`. The verb must not outrun the label. (This is the failure mode that
`A4-FULL-01` produced between 2026-07-22 and 2026-07-25: the label was honestly recorded as
`COMPUTED` under the old definition, which did not distinguish sampling from exhaustion, and
the prose in `RESULTS.md` §5.5 then called it "解決した" with no qualifier.)
