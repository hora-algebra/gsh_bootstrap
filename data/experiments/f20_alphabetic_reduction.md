## What the run establishes

1. **Lemma B is exactly right, and sharp at length 3.** For `C_4`, `C_6` and `S_3` the
   script enumerates *all* maps `G → G` (256, 46,656 and 46,656 respectively) and finds
   that the maps preserving every identity-fibre word of length ≤ 3 are exactly the
   endomorphisms (4, 6 and 10 of them). Length 3 suffices because the forcing argument
   uses only `(e)`, `(g)(g^{-1})` and `(g)(h)((gh)^{-1})`.
2. **Monolithicity across the ladder.** Normal subgroups are enumerated as the subgroups
   generated by unions of conjugacy classes (complete, not a search). `F_20` has exactly
   4 normal subgroups with monolith `C_5`, and every nontrivial normal subgroup contains
   `C_5`. Monolithic: `F_20`, `C_7⋊C_3`, `SL(2,3)`, `S_4`, `A_4`, `D_5`, `C_5`, `C_4`,
   `S_3`. Trivial monolith, hence reducible by Theorem C: `C_2×A_4`, `C_2×S_4`, `Dic_3`,
   `C_6`, `C_2×C_2`, `C_20`.
3. **`Dic_3` as a positive control on already-settled data.** `Dic_3` has normal subgroups
   of order 2 and 3 meeting trivially; the quotients are computed as coset groups and
   identified as the non-abelian group of order 6 (`≅ S_3`, matched by order profile and
   non-commutativity) and `C_4`. Both have order < 12, so Theorem C re-derives
   `DIC3-ALL-01` without the `DIC3-RED-01` embedding.
4. **`C_2×A_4` and `C_2×S_4`.** Both direct factors are verified normal with trivial
   intersection, so Theorem C applies. `C_2×A_4` therefore inherits `A4-ALLLANG-01`
   (`COMPUTED`), and `C_2×S_4` reduces to `S_4` alone.
5. **Theorem C positive control on `C_6`.** The two quotient maps are verified to be
   homomorphisms with kernels of order 3 and 2 meeting trivially, reduced alphabets of
   2 and 3 letters, and `T = mu_1^{-1}(e) ∩ mu_2^{-1}(e)` on all 55,987 words of
   length ≤ 6.
6. **Theorem E on the simple group `C_5`.** The splitting `A = {0,1}`, `B = {0,2,3}` is
   verified injective with `a + b = beta`; neither coordinate is a homomorphism;
   `mu = rho ∘ Φ` and the 5-rectangle union identity hold on all 97,656 words of
   length ≤ 7. `C_5` is verified simple, so Lemma B's homomorphism form is impossible
   there — this is what makes the general form strictly stronger.
7. **Theorem F, the 20 → 8 reduction, with no sampling in the algebra.** All four
   relabelings have the same 8-letter image `Δ = Z/4 × {0,1}`, `Δ` generates `F_20`, none
   of the four is a homomorphism, and the splitting is injective on the 20 letters.
   `H` has 2,500 elements and `rho` is verified to be a homomorphism on **all 6,250,000
   pairs**; it is onto with `|ker rho| = 125`. `lambda` is a section landing in `H`.
   `mu(w) = rho(Φ(w))` on all 168,421 words of length ≤ 4 and on 60,000 random words of
   length 5..40; `T = Φ^{-1}(ker rho)` on all 168,421 words of length ≤ 4.
8. **8 → 7 by erasure.** `Δ` contains the identity and `mu(v) = mu(pi(v))` on all 37,449
   `Δ`-words of length ≤ 5, so the `FULL-ALPH-RED-02` argument drops the obligation to
   7 letters.
9. **The reduction evades neither known obstruction — checked, not assumed.** `Δ` still
   contains the two `ε = 2` letters `(2,0)` and `(2,1)`, so `F20-FULL-OBS-01`'s period-2
   cause survives. The minimal witness of `F20-SUB10-OBS-01` — `k = (0,0)`,
   `u_0 = (1,0)`, `u_1 = (1,1)` — consists entirely of `Δ` letters, and
   `k u_0 u_1 k`, `k u_1 u_0 k` still have distinct images `(2,1)` and `(2,2)` over `Δ`.
   After erasure the witness collapses because `k` is the identity letter, so the
   7-letter instance needs its own test.
10. **Lemma G.** `F_20` is verified *not* to be (abelian)-by-(elementary abelian 2), while
    `C_4`, `C_5`, `C_20`, `C_2×C_2`, `D_5`, `S_3` are; among the 14 subgroups of `F_20`
    only `F_20` itself escapes the property. So at least one coordinate of any reduction
    must be an `F_20` instance over a proper sub-alphabet.
11. **Necessary conditions for pushing further.** The counting bound `∏|Δ_j| ≥ 20` and
    the order-divisibility test `ord(g) | lcm_j ord(f_j(g))` are verified for the achieved
    family. Inside the equal-phase family, all-singleton images are impossible, so
    `max_j |Δ_j| ≥ 8` there and 8 is attained; a variant reaches alphabet sizes 4, 8, 8,
    12 with the 4 abelian. **Subsequent result:** `F20-PHASE-RIGID-01` proves without the
    equal-phase ansatz that some coordinate must retain all four phases, hence every
    factorization of this `F_20`-coordinate type has `max_j |Δ_j| ≥ 4`.
12. **Negative controls.** The verified splitting factors; an all-constant family does not
    (it only sees the word length); 12 of 12 random phase-preserving splittings fail to
    factor, so factoring is a real constraint and not automatic; and a family that keeps
    one coordinate injective factors but gives no reduction (its alphabet is all 20
    letters), which is the control that "factors" alone is not the interesting property.

## Verdict

Route (iv) is **not** blocked. It yields the first genuine alphabet reduction for `F_20`
(20 → 8 → 7, against 19 from `FULL-ALPH-RED-02`), a positive closure theorem that prunes
the frontier by one group. `F20-PHASE-RIGID-01` later closed the proposed 2-letter
successor negatively; it did not close `F_20`.
